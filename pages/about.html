Our Story

Built on Quality,
Driven by Growth

Established in 2008, Hasiria Marketing Sdn Bhd is a dynamic and growing company based in Petaling Jaya, Selangor. We specialise in the sourcing, processing and distribution of high-quality spices and agro-based food products across domestic and international markets.

With a strong foundation in the agro industry, we deliver excellence through a reliable global logistics network, professional repacking services and a relentless commitment to customer satisfaction. Today, we are building active export partnerships across India, Brunei, the Middle East and Africa.

Guided by our vision to become a world-class global enterprise, we are steadily building our presence across India, Brunei, the Middle East and Africa — markets with enormous appetite for the quality Malaysian agro products we trade.
