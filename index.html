Who We Are

A Dynamic & Growing
Agro Trading Company

Established in 2008, Hasiria Marketing Sdn Bhd is a dynamic and growing company specialising in the sourcing, processing and distribution of high-quality spices and agro-based food products. With a strong foundation in the agro industry, we deliver excellence across domestic and international markets.

Our core expertise lies in the import and export of a wide range of agro products, supported by a reliable global logistics network. We also provide professional repacking services for spices, tailored to meet the needs of retail businesses — ensuring product freshness, hygiene and appealing presentation.

FAQs

Import, Export & Spice Sourcing — Common Questions

Answers to what wholesale buyers and distribution partners ask us most about sourcing spices from Malaysia and our import-export logistics.

What spices does Hasiria Marketing export from Malaysia?

We export whole spices and spice powders — including turmeric, black pepper, white pepper, cardamom, cumin, coriander, fennel, star anise, cloves, cinnamon and chili — in bulk, wholesale quantities to buyers in India, Brunei, Singapore, the Middle East and Africa, alongside our Myirfahn (My Irfan) branded spice range.

Do you handle the full import and export process?

Yes. As a licensed Malaysia-based import-export company, we manage sourcing, documentation, customs clearance and shipping so wholesale and B2B partners can import our agro products without handling logistics themselves.

What logistics and shipping options are available for bulk orders?

We coordinate container booking, freight forwarding and customs clearance for sea and air shipments out of Port Klang and Kuala Lumpur, with FOB and CIF terms available depending on the destination country and order volume.

Is Hasiria Marketing a halal-certified supplier?

Yes, our spices and agro-food products are sourced and processed to halal food-grade standards, making us a trusted supplier for hypermarkets, restaurant chains and distributors across Muslim-majority export markets.

What is the minimum order quantity for wholesale spice exports?

Minimum order quantities vary by product and destination — get in touch with our team via the contact form below and we'll confirm pricing, MOQs and lead times for your specific spice or agro-product requirement.
